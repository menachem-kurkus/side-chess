# Credits: the Celtic pack's pieces

The piece drawings are **not ours**. They are the `celtic` set from the Lichess project's
piece collection (https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/celtic),
as listed in its COPYING.md.

- Author: Maurizio Monge (https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art)
- Licence: MIT
- What we changed: the twelve SVG drawings were rendered to 128 px PNG tiles and laid out as
  one 768 x 256 `pieces.png` (King Queen Rook Bishop Knight Pawn across, white row then black row).
  The board colours in `pack.txt` are ours.
