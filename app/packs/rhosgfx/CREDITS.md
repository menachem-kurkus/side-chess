# Credits: the Peach pack's pieces

The piece drawings are **not ours**. They are the `rhosgfx` set from the Lichess project's
piece collection (https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/rhosgfx),
as listed in its COPYING.md.

- Author: RhosGFX (https://rhosgfx.itch.io/)
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- What we changed: the twelve SVG drawings were rendered to 128 px PNG tiles and laid out as
  one 768 x 256 `pieces.png` (King Queen Rook Bishop Knight Pawn across, white row then black row).
  The board colours in `pack.txt` are ours.
